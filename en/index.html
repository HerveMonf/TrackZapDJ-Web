Features

No more duplicates cluttering your sets

TrackZapDJ scans your collection, shows you the duplicates it finds clearly, and lets you decide.

🔍

Smart detection

Groups tracks with the same artist and title, with a confidence level for each group.

🎼

Musical analysis

Also uncovers duplicates hiding behind different names, using key, BPM and duration.

🔐

Bit-for-bit verification

Compares the actual file contents to confirm they are strictly identical.

⭐

Best version suggested

Suggests which track to keep based on your cues, your playlists and audio quality. You have the final say.

🎚️

Playlists, history, Automix, tags and cues

During cleanup, your playlists, your history and your Automix list point to the kept track, so you can still replay a past set. Your tags are transferred, and your hot cues / memory cues recovered whenever possible.

📊

Detailed report

Export all the duplicate analysis details to an Excel file, even in the free version. During cleanup, every operation performed is recorded in a log file.

🛡️ Built with caution

A careful cleanup, every step of the way

Your library is the result of years of work. TrackZapDJ adds safeguards before, during and after the cleanup.

👀

Scanning changes nothing

Scanning and reviewing the plan leaves your database and your files untouched. That's the whole idea of the free version.

💾

Automatic backup

Before any change, your database, playlists and Automix list are copied to the backup folder you choose.

🧪

Simulation mode

See exactly what would be done, without changing a single piece of data.

♻️

One-click undo

Removed audio files are moved to your backup folder, not deleted. From the history, go back to the state before any of your last 5 cleanups: database and files return to their place.

✋

Manual approval

Nothing is deleted without your explicit approval, group by group. Checks block the cleanup while your DJ software is open, and a file still in use is never deleted.

🏠

100% local

Everything is processed on your computer. Your library is never sent anywhere.

To be honest: these safeguards greatly reduce the risks, but no software can remove them entirely. Before a big cleanup, keep your own backup of your library and your files too.

FAQ

You might be wondering…

What's the difference between the free and the paid version?

The free version scans your library, shows the duplicates, prepares an action plan and lets you export the full analysis to an Excel file, but doesn't make any changes. The paid version will add the ability to carry out that plan: backing up the database and files to the folder of your choice, one-click undo, redirecting playlists, history and the Automix list to the kept track, transferring tags.

How much will the paid version cost? Is there a subscription?

No subscription: it will be a one-time purchase. The planned price for the first paid version is €3.99. At a later stage, it may rise to €5.99 for new buyers.

When will TrackZapDJ be available?

The free version comes first: it's planned to launch in early November. The paid version will follow later; it can't be purchased yet. Drop us an email to be notified.

The free version is available now. The paid version will follow later; it can't be purchased yet.

Does the free version change my library?

No. It reads your library to analyse it and shows you what could be done. No database and no file is modified.

Are my playlists, history, Automix, tags and cues preserved?

That's one of the things we watch most closely. During cleanup, your playlists, your history and your Automix list point to the kept track: your history stays complete and you can replay a past set. Your tags are transferred to the kept track, and your hot cues / memory cues are recovered whenever possible. The suggested track to keep takes your cues and playlists into account, and you can always pick a different one.

Could I lose data?

TrackZapDJ is designed to limit that risk: automatic backup, simulation mode, manual approval, files set aside rather than deleted, and one-click undo. Still, no tool can guarantee zero risk, so keep your own backup before a big cleanup.

Is my library sent over the Internet?

No. The whole analysis runs on your computer. Your tracks and your database stay with you.

Which DJ software is supported?

Rekordbox 6.x and 7.x at launch. Serato DJ and VirtualDJ are planned next.

Does TrackZapDJ run on Mac?

Not yet. The first version is available on PC only. The app is designed to run on both PC and Mac: the Mac version will follow.
