FAQ

The questions people ask me

Are my cues and playlists safe?

Yes. Playlists, history, Automix and tags move to the kept copy. Hot cues and memory cues are copied over when both files have the same duration (within 1 second), without duplicates. And your database is backed up before every cleanup, so you can always roll back.

Does everything stay on my PC?

Yes, nothing is sent over the Internet. Removed files aren't erased: they go to your backup folder, and are only deleted once they drop out of your last 5 cleanups. TrackZapDJ asks you first.

Is there a subscription?

No. The scan is free, and the cleanup costs €3.99, once and for all.

What does it work with?

Rekordbox 6.x and 7.x, on PC. TrackZapDJ scans the tracks in your Rekordbox collection. Mac, Serato, VirtualDJ and Traktor are planned. The app is in French for now; English is coming for the official launch.

The TrackZapDJ story, in 30 seconds

I'm Hervé, the founder of TrackZapDJ. I started DJing in clubs in the late '80s, on vinyl and our beloved MK2s: New Wave first, then House, New Beat and Electro. I never really let go of the decks, even when my career took over: I stayed a passionate amateur.

During Covid, I finally switched to a controller — a DDJ-1000 with Rekordbox. Gigabytes of MP3s piled up from all kinds of sources, and my digital collection filled up with duplicates.

There are full software suites, packed with features, that also handle duplicates. But for my use, they were too expensive and didn't quite fit this particular need. So I built TrackZapDJ. It's an independent project: the free version is meant to be useful on its own, and purchases pay for hosting, maintenance and new features.
