What TrackZapDJ will do

No more duplicates cluttering your sets

TrackZapDJ scans your collection, shows you clearly which duplicates it found, and lets you decide.

🔍

Smart detection

Groups tracks with the same artist and title, with a confidence level for each group.

🎼

Musical analysis

Also catches duplicates hiding behind different names, using key, BPM and duration.

🔐

Bit-for-bit verification

Compares the actual file contents to confirm they are strictly identical.

⭐

Best version suggested

Automatically suggests which track to keep based on your cues, playlists and audio quality. You always have the final say.

🎚️

Playlists and cues preserved

Your playlists point to the track you keep, and your hot cues and memory cues are carried over whenever possible.

📊

Detailed report

Export the list of duplicates and keep a full record of every operation performed.

🛡️ Built to lose nothing

A safe cleanup, every step of the way

Your library is the result of years of work. TrackZapDJ stacks up safeguards before, during and after every cleanup.

💾

Automatic backup

Before any change, your database is copied to a timestamped backup.

🧪

Dry-run mode

See exactly what would be done, without changing a single byte of data.

♻️

Trash, not permanent deletion

Audio files go to your system's trash, so you can always restore them.

✋

Manual approval

Nothing is deleted without your explicit approval, group by group.

🚦

Safety checks

Cleanup is blocked while your DJ software is open, and a file that is still in use is never deleted.

🏠

100% local

Everything runs on your computer. Your library is never uploaded anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

When will TrackZapDJ be available?

The app is being finalized. Leave us your email address and you'll be among the first to know when it launches.

Could I lose my playlists or cues?

TrackZapDJ is designed precisely to prevent that: automatic backup, dry-run mode, manual approval, and files sent to the trash rather than deleted.

No. All the analysis happens on your computer. Your tracks and your database stay with you.
