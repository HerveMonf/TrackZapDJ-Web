Terms of sale

Being written

Privacy policy

Being written

Until the full text is ready, here is what I can already say:

  • TrackZapDJ analyses your library on your own computer. Your library is never sent anywhere.
  • Licence activation (paid version): TrackZapDJ sends the TrackZapDJ server, once, the key, an anonymous fingerprint of the computer (neither its name nor yours) and the version number. The server keeps the fingerprint to limit the licence to one computer.
  • Purchase: payment is processed by Stripe. The TrackZapDJ server keeps the buyer's e-mail address with their key, only to be able to send it again if it is lost.
  • On this website, a "lang" cookie remembers the language you chose, for one year.
  • If you email me, your address is only used to reply to you, or to let you know when TrackZapDJ is released if you asked me to.
